# Topic Options

## Main Topic

**Design and Implementation of a Maternal Health Decision Support System for Primary Health Centre Staff to Flag High-Risk Pregnant Women Using Simple Clinic Records in Ugbomoro**

## Backup Titles

1. **Design and Implementation of a Maternal Risk Screening Dashboard for Primary Health Centre Staff in Ugbomoro**
2. **Design and Implementation of a Maternal Care Decision Support Platform for Identifying High-Risk Pregnancies in Ugbomoro**
3. **Design and Implementation of a Pregnancy Risk Flagging System for Primary Health Centres in Ugbomoro**

## Why This Topic Works

- It is clearly a system project, not just an AI model.
- It solves a real health problem in a local setting.
- It can be built with simple clinic data and standard machine learning tools.
- It is easy to explain as a decision support tool, not a diagnosis tool.
